Implemented: User-Controlled Walking with Automatic Corner Steering

The navigation has been upgraded so that the 3D character no longer walks by himself automatically. The character now moves strictly when you walk, and automatically takes every turn along the corridor with you:

---

How It Works

1. User-Driven Movement (No Ghost Auto-Walking):

  • When you select a destination, the 3D character and cyan route ribbon appear, but the character stands in place waiting for you.

  • You can walk with the character in three natural ways:

  • On Desktop/Laptop: Hold the W key or Up Arrow.

  • On Mobile/Touchscreen: Push the Virtual Joystick forward.

  • On Both: Press & hold the on-screen "Hold to Walk" button in the navigation card.

  • As soon as you release the key, joystick, or button, the character stops immediately and stands in place with natural breathing.

2. Automatic Corner & Turn Steering:
  • As you walk forward along the route, the engine dynamically calculates the corridor curve ahead.

  • When you approach a corner (e.g., turning left into the Workshop or right towards the 3D Printers), the 3D character automatically steers into the turn and continues down the corridor.

  • The camera smoothly follows behind in 3D Follow View, rotating through the corner with the character.

  • The HUD instructions update in real-time ("Turn left into Workshop in 3m" → "Walk straight 12m").

3. Real Walking Speed & Gait Animation:
  • The stride cadence matches real human walking speed (1.45 m/s).

  • The animated legs, swinging arms, head bobbing, and floor footstep ripple rings only animate while you are actively walking.

4. Autopilot Preview (Optional):
  • If you ever want a hands-free preview glide without holding controls, you can toggle the small "Auto-Glide" button on the card.

Here is a breakdown of the exact changes made to implement user-controlled walking with automatic corner steering:

---

1. Stopped Automatic "Ghost" Walking ([autoWalk.ts](file:///d:/Namaspace/src/navigation/autoWalk.ts))

  • Previous behavior: As soon as a destination was picked, the character started gliding along the path on every frame without waiting for user input.
  • What changed:
  • Added a check: const isActivelyWalking = isAutoWalking || isUserWalking;.
  • If you are not pressing W, not pushing the joystick, and not holding the Walk button, the position freezes in place. The character stands naturally and waits for you.

---

2. Automatic Corner & Turn Steering ([autoWalk.ts](file:///d:/Namaspace/src/navigation/autoWalk.ts))

  • How it works:
  • While you walk, the engine computes a look-ahead vector along the hallway curve:
    const lookAheadPt = curveRef.current.getPointAt(lookAheadDistance);
    const targetYaw = Math.atan2(-(lookAheadPt.x - currentPt.x), -(lookAheadPt.z - currentPt.z));
  • It continuously interpolates the character's heading userRotation into that target angle using shortest-angle rotation interpolation.
  • Result: As you walk forward into a corner (e.g., turning 90° into the Workshop or Laser Cabin), the 3D character automatically steers through the turn, and the follow camera turns behind him without hitting any walls.

---

3. Integrated Keyboard & Touch Joystick Controls

  • Desktop Keyboard ([FirstPersonControls.tsx](file:///d:/Namaspace/src/roaming/FirstPersonControls.tsx)):
  • Pressing or holding W or ArrowUp triggers setIsUserWalking(true). Releasing them immediately sets it to false.
  • Mobile Virtual Joystick ([VirtualJoystick.tsx](file:///d:/Namaspace/src/roaming/VirtualJoystick.tsx)):
  • Pushing the joystick forward (normY > 0.2) triggers setIsUserWalking(true). Releasing the joystick sets it to false.
  • Removed the lock that previously disabled the joystick during navigation, so mobile users can walk with their thumb.

---

4. Added Tactile "Hold to Walk" Button ([DirectionHUD.tsx](file:///d:/Namaspace/src/ui/DirectionHUD.tsx))

  • Added a primary button that supports both mouse and touch holding:
  • Holding down: Activates walking, plays the animated stride icon, and displays "Walking with you • Turning corners...".
  • Releasing: Instantly halts the character in place.
  • Added a secondary "Auto-Glide" button for optional hands-free simulation.

---

5. Walking Animations Synced with the User ([NavigationPuck.tsx](file:///d:/Namaspace/src/scene/NavigationPuck.tsx))

  • Connected the 3D character's leg swings, arm swings, body bobbing, and floor footstep ripple rings directly to isUserWalking.
  • When you stop walking, the legs and arms immediately return to a natural standing stance with gentle idle breathing.

---

6. State Management Updates ([useStore.ts](file:///d:/Namaspace/src/state/useStore.ts))

  • Added isUserWalking: boolean, isNavigating: boolean, and navigationProgress: number.
  • setNavigationRoute now initializes isUserWalking: false so that selecting any destination waits for the user to initiate movement.
